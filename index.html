How it works

The same pipeline that produced the detection above

Today it runs on a laptop against downloaded imagery. On a flying satellite, the identical logic runs on a Hailo-8 or Jetson Orin chip bolted to the sensor — milliseconds after capture, no download step.

01 · CAPTURE

Sensor reads the scene

Optical and near-infrared bands record reflectance. Water absorbs near-infrared almost completely — vessels don't, which makes them stand out as sharp outliers.

02 · INFER

Edge AI runs on-orbit

A statistical outlier model isolates real targets from noise and texture. A second pass measures sub-pixel shift between spectral bands to estimate speed and heading — from one image, no video.

03 · DELIVER

Structured alert, not an image

Position, size, velocity, AIS cross-reference, confidence — a few kilobytes, sent down in minutes through the API, instead of gigabytes for someone to review later.
